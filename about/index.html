The story

On the Dot started at a kitchen table in Toronto, with a simple frustration: life runs on a hundred little things — bills, paydays, applications, pickup runs — and the apps that exist only ever solved one piece at a time, the same way for everybody. So we started building our own: small, honest web apps that do one everyday job properly, each with something the others don't have. No clones. No noise. Just tools that show up on time — on the dot.
