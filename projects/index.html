Project #1 · In development

Household Finance Tracker

Working title — the final name is still being chosen.

One clear picture of your household's money: every account, every bill, and the whole family's month — in, out, and what's left.

  • Add income and expenses in seconds
  • See each account's flow, plus the whole household's in / out / net
  • Transfers between your own accounts kept in their own ledger — never counted as income or spending
  • E-transfers confirmed before they're booked, so nothing gets counted twice
  • A month-end verdict: how did we do?
Our edge: most money apps blur transfers into spending and leave you guessing. Ours keeps them separate, so your numbers tell the truth.

Just your email, used only to tell you when it's ready. We never sell or share it.
